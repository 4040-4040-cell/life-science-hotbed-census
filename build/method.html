Inclusion rule. A company is listed if it has an HQ, R&D or manufacturing site in the region and meets one test: T1 exchange-listed and headquartered in the region; T2 private with $100M+ raised lifetime or $50M+ since October 2023; T3 100+ regional employees; T4 an approved or cleared product (FDA; MHRA or EMA for UK companies; Swissmedic, EMA or FDA for Basel); T5 a big pharma, tools or CDMO site with 100+ employees, 100,000+ SF or a named plant; T6 anchor institution or incubator; T7 private company with $10M+ sold in SEC Form D offerings since January 2023 (issuer address from the filing; screened for acquisitions, closures and out-of-scope device or consumer companies). Companies acquired, merged or closed as of October 28, 2026 are excluded. Basel (the trinational area of Basel-Stadt, Basel-Landschaft, the Aargau Fricktal, northern Solothurn, Jura, southern Alsace and South Baden) uses the same tests, with T1 covering SIX, Euronext and Xetra listings and T7 replaced by $10M+ of disclosed equity financing since January 2023, because Swiss companies do not file Form D. Swiss francs and euros are converted to dollars at the rate on the announcement date. Basel exclusions are as of November 2, 2026.

Score (analyst assumption, not a market standard). Each company gets 1 to 5 on five measures, weighted: scale {WS}% (employees; revenue where headcount is missing), capital {WC}% (public float from the latest 10-K cover, or private capital raised), cash durability {WD}% (months of cash at the last fiscal year's operating burn; recency of last round for privates), stage {WT}% (approved product, else highest active trial phase), momentum {WM}% (footprint expansion or contraction, financing in the last 12 months, approval in the last 24 months). Tier A is 3.80 and above, Tier B 2.60 to 3.79, Tier C below 2.60. A measure with no sourced data is dropped and the weights rescale; an asterisk marks scores built on less than 60% of the weight. Sites of large companies inherit the parent's capital and durability.

Read. Each company carries one read. Expansion candidate: Tier A or B with an expansion event, a raise in the last 12 months or headcount up 10%+, and either positive operating cash flow or 24+ months of cash. Contraction risk: a contraction event, under 12 months of cash, or headcount down 10%+. Watch: a Phase 2 or 3 readout expected within 12 months. No signal: none of these was found. That is an absence of a signal, not a finding that the footprint is stable. Sites of outside companies read only from their own footprint event. Footprint events are hand-classified from filings and releases dated 2025 to 2026.

Sources. Company websites and filings (SEC EDGAR 10-K, 10-Q and Form D), openFDA Drugs@FDA bulk file (updated October 28, 2026; covers CDER drugs and biologics, not CBER cell, gene and vaccine approvals), openFDA device 510(k) and PMA, ClinicalTrials.gov v2 (lead sponsor, studies active or completed since 2021), company press releases, BioSpace hotbed company lists (BioSpace's nicknames Genetown, Biotech Bay, Biotech Beach, Pharm Country, BioCapital, Bio NC and BioMidwest map to the geographies used here), regional trade association reports. Employee counts from 10-Ks are company-wide, not regional. UK companies are not SEC filers unless US-listed, so their capital and durability measures are thinner. Logos are taken from each company's own website for identification and link to it. Form D additions whose filing address is a mailbox, an apartment or an office suite shared by three or more filers (a venture firm's office) are listed but not mapped. Other Form D additions take the submarket of their three nearest mapped neighbors within 1.5 km, otherwise the submarket of their city. Locations geocoded with OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and Swiss addresses with the federal building register (geo.admin.ch); Basel addresses are taken from the Swiss commercial register (Zefix) or company contact pages; cash for Basel companies is shown only for SEC filers (Novartis 20-F, BeOne 10-K); faded pins sit at city level because no street address was verified. Aerial imagery and place labels: Esri World Imagery (Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, USDA, USGS); capture dates vary by tile and may predate recent construction.
